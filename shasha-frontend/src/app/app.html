The Atelier Process

Every garment begins with bespoke client measurement, ancestral Toghu needlework, and multiple private fittings until perfection is achieved.

01

Private Consultation

In-person fitting in our Douala atelier or remote digital silhouette consultation with fabric swatch courier worldwide.

02

Artisan Hand-Embroidery

Between 180 and 240 hours of meticulous hand-placed beads, seed pearls, and sacred Grassfields Toghu geometry on velvet and pure silk.

03

White-Glove Delivery

Finished creations packaged in custom archival garment bags with direct insured courier to Douala, Yaoundé, Paris, London, or Atlanta.
